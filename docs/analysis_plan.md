# Analysis protocol: official NCAA attendance

Frozen before model evaluation, 2026-11-01. Replace generated CRM/ticket records with official NCAA men's and women's basketball attendance records through 2025-26. Source bytes are locked by SHA-256; parsing records PDF page and column. Publish derived factual aggregates with attribution, not the complete copyrighted PDFs.

Primary descriptive scope: Division I school home attendance (school/sex/season), plus reported divisional historical averages and totals. Exclude ranked leader duplicates and separately labelled reclassifying schools from the main school table. Check school count, home games and attendance against the source summary. The source women's summary includes reclassifying attendance in its totals while its team count excludes those schools; retain both scopes separately and require this difference to reconcile exactly to the four reclassifying rows. Rounded official averages may disagree with recomputed averages by up to one attendee; retain both. Do not conflate overall division totals including neutral games with home attendance. Any unexplained failed reconciliation blocks publication. Known source discrepancies must be independently quantified, visible and excluded from unsupported conclusions.

Historical forecast: predict next reported men's Division I all-games average attendance, not individual ticket purchases, revenue, or customer retention. Compare last-year baseline, trailing three observed-season average, and a fixed regularized one-lag Ridge model. Use only prior-calendar-year available features; no bridging the missing 2021 official season. Primary rolling-origin test years 2023-2026, train ends strictly before each test year, minimum ten past observations. Restrict forecasting to the men's Division I all-games series after source inspection found incompatible female 2025 average fields and mixed scope in other division headers. Fit one-lag average, lagged team count and linear time with training-only standardization, alpha fixed at 10, no test-driven tuning. Report MAE, median absolute percentage error and each origin; retain baseline wins. Note these are retrospective backtests on a current revised record book, not archived vintage forecasts. Limited aggregate sample and post-COVID breaks constrain conclusions.

Also measure real SQL reporting query equivalence with materialization construction cost, warmups and interleaved timings; make no fixed speedup promise. Include source coverage, gaps, SQL/pandas reconciliation, rank concentration and gender-specific comparisons on common seasons. Do not infer people, paid sales, capacity utilization or campaign impact from attendance.

Source-audit scope amendment before any model evaluation: women's ordinary Division I home list and four reclassifying rows are separate cohorts. Main school cards use the ordinary list; the official summary is shown separately. Men's historical Division III header and women's 2025 all-games averages have inconsistencies, so they are excluded from the forecast. Forecast testing has only four annual origins, so it is an exploratory baseline comparison, not evidence of deployment readiness.
